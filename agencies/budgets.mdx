## Budgets per brand

<Info>Business plan.</Info>

Give each brand a budget for AI credits, crawls and email validations, per
day, week or month. A brand's budget counts everything done in it, background
agents included. When it's spent, that work stops in the brand until the
period resets; other brands carry on.

## Usage report

Each brand's usage report shows, month by month, its AI credits, crawls,
validations, emails sent and call minutes. Download it as CSV
(`GET /api/brands/{id}/budget?format=csv`, needs the `export` action) to
invoice the client.

<Note>ManyPI bills your workspace. Invoicing your clients stays with you.</Note>

## Partner program

Apply at [manypi.com/partners](https://manypi.com/partners) as an agency,
technology partner or affiliate. Every application is reviewed by hand.

- **Agency partners** can be listed in the
  [agency directory](https://manypi.com/partners/agencies) with a profile and
  a badge for their site: **Partner**, **Certified** (proven client results)
  or **Elite** (most active).
- **Affiliates** get a personal link. Commission is agreed with each one.

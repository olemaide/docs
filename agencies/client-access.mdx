---
title: "Client access"
description: "Let clients see their results, or reply and edit, without seeing your other clients."
---

Invite people under **Settings → Team**. For the roles below, pick the brands
they may see; they never see another brand. Invites are valid for 7 days.

| Role | What they can do | Seat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client | See their leads, campaigns, inbox, CRM, calls and sites. Change nothing. | Free |
| Client editor | Also reply from the inbox and edit their CRM and sites | Paid |
| Freelancer | Do the work in their brands, including sending and publishing; no team or settings | Paid |
| Viewer | See everything in their brands, change nothing | Free |

Roles that only look are free, up to 100 per workspace. Nobody can invite
someone with more rights than their own. Owners and admins are never limited
to brands.

<Info>
  Inviting clients, per-brand roles, "own records only" and per-member usage
  limits are on Pro and Business. Custom roles are on Business.
</Info>

Every role and permission: [Roles and permissions](/platform/roles-and-permissions).

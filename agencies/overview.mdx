---

Your **workspace** is your agency: one bill, one team, one pool of credits.
Each client gets a **brand**. A brand holds that client's positioning,
sending inboxes, campaigns, forms, landing pages, phone lines and
integrations, so switching brands switches the app to that client.

## Set up a client

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the brand">
    Open **Brands** and add the client's name, website and positioning. The
    agent uses it in every search and draft for that brand. See
    [Brands and teams](/platform/brands-and-teams).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect their sending">
    Switch to the brand and connect the client's [inboxes](/outreach/inboxes)
    and, for calls, their [phone line](/calls/overview#connect-a-phone-line).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect their domain">
    Add it under **Domains** so their pages and forms live on it. See
    [Custom domains](/pages/custom-domains).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do the work">
    Find or import leads, build campaigns, landing pages and forms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite the client">
    Limited to their brand. See [Client access](/agencies/client-access).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Leads and CRM deals belong to the workspace and are filtered by brand. A lead
  added without a brand shows up in every brand.
</Note>

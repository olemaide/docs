Every call keeps a **transcript**, a written **summary** and an **outcome**.
Calls are transcribed, not recorded.

## Outcomes

Meeting agreed · Interested · Call back later · Not interested · Asked not to
be called · Wrong person · Voicemail · Nobody spoke · No answer · Did not
connect

## On the lead

- The summary is added as a CRM note.
- The lead gets three fields you can filter by or merge into emails:
  `call_outcome`, `call_at` and `call_summary`.
- Someone who asks not to be called goes on **Do not call**.

## Follow-up, switched on under Settings → After a call

| Toggle | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Create a deal | For an interested or meeting outcome. Never a second open deal for the same lead. |
| Book the meeting | When they agree to a time the agent offered. Needs [Google Calendar](/integrations/google-calendar), [Cal.com](/integrations/calcom) or [Calendly](/integrations/calendly). Any other time becomes a task. |
| Call back when asked | At the time they name, at most twice in a row and within 60 days. Otherwise a task. |

## Voicemail

On a callback the agent leaves one short message. A proactive call leaves none.

## Events

`call.completed` (**A call ends**) fires for [workflows](/agent/workflows) and
[webhooks](/integrations/webhooks).

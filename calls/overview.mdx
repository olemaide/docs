<Info>AI calls are in beta and on paid plans.</Info>

**Calls** in the sidebar puts an AI agent on the phone for you. It calls leads
who asked to be called, works towards a goal you set (book a meeting, check
interest), offers times from your calendar, and leaves a transcript, a summary
and an outcome on the lead.

## Set up

Until the workspace is ready, the Calls page shows three steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept the calling terms">
    An owner or admin reads and accepts them once for the workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a phone line">
    Bring a number from your SIP provider. It is the caller ID people see. See
    [Connect a phone line](#connect-a-phone-line).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add call minutes">
    Buy a minute pack, or switch on pay as you go. See [Pricing](#pricing).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect a phone line

You bring the line and pay your provider for it. The AI voice is included in
ManyPI, so you need no voice-provider account.

Under **Phone lines**, click **Connect a line**:

| Step | What you enter |
| --- | --- |
| Provider | **Twilio** (Elastic SIP Trunking), **Telnyx** or **Another provider**. The trunk must support TLS and a username and password. |
| Trunk | **Trunk address**, **Username**, **Password**. **Where do I find these?** shows where your provider keeps them. |
| Number | The **Phone number**, with `+` and the country code, and an optional name. |
| Answering | **Only place calls**, or **Answer calls too**. |

With **Answer calls too**, describe what the agent should do for a caller,
point the number's calls at the SIP address shown under **Send the number's
calls here**, and choose whether it answers **Always** or **In my calling
hours**. Callers who withhold their number are not answered.

Only owners and admins can connect lines.

## Teach the agent

Under **Settings**:

| Section | What you set |
| --- | --- |
| The agent | Company name, voice (11 to choose from), longest call (1–30 minutes, default 5), calls a day (1–2,000, default 50). |
| What it knows | Up to 20,000 characters about your offer. Switch on **Brand profile** to use the brand, and **Meeting times** to offer slots from your calendar (meeting length 10–240 minutes). |
| Calling hours | When calls may go out. See [Calling rules](/calls/rules). |
| After a call | Deals, bookings and callbacks. See [After a call](/calls/after-a-call). |

Lines, settings and what the agent knows belong to one brand, so each brand
gets its own number and its own pitch.

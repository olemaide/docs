## One call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open New call">
    Click **New call** and stay on **One call**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick who">
    Search for a lead, or type a number like `+49 30 1234567`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say why you may call">
    **A callback** when they asked you to call. **Proactive** when you are
    calling first, with their **consent** as the basis.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the goal">
    **What the call is for** is a goal, not a script. Start from **Book a
    meeting**, **Check interest** or **Preview follow-up**, then pick the
    language: **theirs**, or a fixed one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the consent and place it">
    Note where they asked and where that is kept, tick the confirmation, and
    click **Place call**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The call is queued right away, or for the next opening in your
[calling hours](/calls/rules#calling-hours). Every call opens with the same
line: *"This is an AI assistant calling for {company}, and this call is
transcribed."*

## Several leads

Select up to 25 leads in the leads table and click **Call with AI**. The call
form opens with all of them, and the button reads **Call N**. Leads that can't
be called yet are skipped with the reason (for example a missing location),
and the toast says how many were queued.

## Automations

Switch **New call** to **Automation** and pick a trigger:

- A form is submitted
- A lead replies, interested
- A campaign ends, no reply
- A deal reaches a stage
- A lead is added

Add an optional **Wait** (1 hour, 4 hours, 1 day, 3 days, or 1–72 hours /
1–30 days), write the goal, and click **Turn on**. Automations can be paused,
and each one is also an ordinary workflow (**Open in Workflows**).

## From a form

Add a **Call consent** block to a [form](/forms/overview) and turn on **Call
them automatically**. Whoever ticks it is called within your calling hours.

| Limit | |
| --- | --- |
| Automatic form calls | 20 per workspace per hour |
| Per number | One per 7 days |

## From the agent

```text
Call Jana Weber from the Berlin dental list and book a 20-minute demo next week.
```

The agent's `calls_place` tool calls one saved lead at a time and asks you to
approve every call. `calls_status`, `calls_list`, `calls_get` and
`calls_cancel` read and cancel calls.

## The calls list

Filter by **All**, **Outgoing**, **Incoming** or **Waiting**, or search. A call
shows its **Transcript**, its **Brief** (or **Instructions** for an incoming
call) and the **Consent** it was placed on. The row menu has **Cancel this
call** (while it waits) and **Never call this number**.

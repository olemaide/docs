---
title: "Calling rules"
description: "Calling hours, consent, how often a number is called, and do not call."
---

ManyPI places calls people asked for. Every call says it is an AI and that it
is transcribed.

## Consent

- **Callbacks**, where the person asked you to call, are always allowed.
- **Proactive** calls need the person's recorded consent. You note where they
  gave it and where it is kept, and confirm it before the call is placed.

## Calling hours

Under **Settings → Calling hours**, choose **Business hours**, **Weekday
mornings**, **As wide as allowed**, or your own **From** and **Until**, and
whether they follow **their time** or **my time**.

Whatever you set, calls stay within:

| Where | Hours |
| --- | --- |
| Everywhere | 8:00–20:00 in the called person's time, never on Sunday |
| France | Weekdays, 10:00–20:00 |

A call outside these hours waits for the next opening.

<Note>
  ManyPI needs the lead's location to know their local time. Without one, the
  call is not placed: *"Add the lead's location."*
</Note>

## How often

| Rule | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Any number | One call per 24 hours |
| Proactive calls | Three per number per 30 days |
| Redials | None automatic |
| Calls a day | Your setting, 1–2,000 (incoming calls don't count) |

## Do not call

Anyone who asks not to be called is added to **Do not call** automatically.
Add numbers yourself in **Settings**, or from a call with **Never call this
number**. Premium-rate numbers are never dialled.

## Not allowed

AI calls may not be used for emergency, debt collection, political or health
calls.

<Warning>
  This is not legal advice. Calling rules differ by country; know which apply
  to the people you call.
</Warning>

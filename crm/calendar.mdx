The calendar puts everything with a date in one place: what you booked, what
is due, what should close and when your campaigns send.

## Open it

| Where | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **CRM → Calendar** | The full calendar: month, week, day or agenda |
| The calendar button in the CRM header, the Inbox footer or the Outreach campaigns toolbar | The next 7 days beside what you're doing |
| Command palette (Ctrl K / Cmd K), type "Calendar" | The same panel, from anywhere |

The panel follows **Settings → Open records**: a column beside the page, or a
drawer. On a phone, the full calendar opens as an agenda.

## What's on it

| Layer | Shows | Click opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meetings | Meetings booked through Google Calendar, Cal.com, Calendly, Zoom or Whereby, with a **Join** button | The contact |
| My calendar | Your own Google Calendar events. Only you see them | The event in Google Calendar |
| Tasks | Tasks on their due date (late ones in red) | The task |
| Deal close dates | Open deals on their expected close date, won and lost deals on the day they closed | The deal |
| Campaigns | The days a campaign sent and the days it's scheduled to send, with the numbers | The campaign |
| AI calls | Scheduled and placed calls | The contact, or Calls |
| Forms closing | The moment a form stops taking responses (off by default) | The form |
| Scheduled workflows | A scheduled workflow's next run (off by default) | The workflow |

Turn layers on and off under **Display**, where **Only mine** also hides what
belongs to teammates. Your choice is remembered on this device.

You only see what you can already see elsewhere in ManyPI: the brands you work
in, your role's areas, and your plan (tasks and deals need the CRM, campaigns
need outreach).

## Move things

Drag a **task** to another day to change its due date, or stretch it to give
it a start date. Drag an **open deal** to change its expected close date.
Meetings, campaigns and calls don't move from the calendar: moving a meeting
emails everyone invited, so do that in the meeting itself.

## My calendar

Connect [Google Calendar](/integrations/google-calendar) to see your own
events next to everything else. They are read when you open the calendar and
never stored, and nobody else in the workspace sees them. Meetings with
outside guests already appear under **Meetings**, so they aren't shown twice,
and time marked as free in Google isn't shown at all.

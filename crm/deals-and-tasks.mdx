## The deal panel

| Tab | |
| --- | --- |
| Activity | The deal's history. Log a call (Connected, No answer, Left a voicemail) or a meeting. |
| Emails | The conversation with the contact (when they have an email) |
| Tasks | What's still to do |
| Notes | What your team wrote down |

Emails with a contact attach to their most recently active open deal.

## Tasks

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Kinds | To-do, call, email, meeting, follow-up |
| Status | Not started, in progress, done |
| Repeat | Daily, weekdays, weekly, every two weeks, monthly, quarterly, yearly |
| Views | List, Board, Calendar, Timeline, Workload |

Tasks also show on the [calendar](/crm/calendar), next to meetings, deal close dates and campaign days.

## AI deal brief

<Info>Pro and Business. No credits; up to 200 per workspace per day.</Info>

A summary of where the deal stands and what to do next, from its emails, notes
and activity.

## From the agent

The agent can read the board, list and change deals and tasks, add notes, list
companies, read a deal's timeline, and book, cancel or reschedule meetings. It
never deletes a deal.

```text
Make a follow-up task for every deal in Proposal with no activity for two weeks.
```

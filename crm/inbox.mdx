---

**Inbox** brings the replies from all your connected email accounts into one
list, so you never log into ten mailboxes to find one interested lead. The
list refreshes every minute while it's open.

## Folders

| Group | Folders |
| --- | --- |
| Yours | Waiting on you, Unread, Assigned to me (teams) |
| Sorted by AI (Pro, Business) | Interested, Objections, Not interested, Out of office |
| Everything | All mail, Replies, Sent no reply, Bounces, Drafts, Snoozed |

## Actions

- Reply, reply all, forward. Sending needs a paid plan.
- Mark **done**, **snooze** (Later today, Tomorrow morning, Next week),
  **assign** to a teammate, mark unread.
- Select up to 250 threads at once.
- Turn a reply into a [CRM deal](/crm/pipeline) with one click.

## AI sorting

<Info>Pro and Business. No credits.</Info>

Each reply is classified as `positive`, `neutral`, `negative`, `objection`,
`unsubscribe`, `ooo`, `referral`, `automated` or `informational`. **Not
right?** corrects it.

What it does to the sequence by default:

| Reply | Sequence |
| --- | --- |
| Unsubscribe | Stops; the address goes on Do not contact |
| Interested, not interested, objection | Stops |
| Out of office, automated | Continues |

## Drafted replies

**Draft** writes an answer into the composer. It never sends by itself. No
credits; up to 100 per workspace per day.

## The reply agent

Give the agent the objective **Answer replies** and an autonomy level:

| Level | |
| --- | --- |
| Suggest | Drafts for you to send |
| Manual | Waits for your go-ahead |
| Auto | Sends within the allowed categories (positive, objection, referral and neutral by default) |

## Mailbox sync

<Info>Pro and Business.</Info>

Switch on per inbox under **CRM → gear → Mail**. The mailbox's other
conversations with your contacts appear here and on their deals.

## Alerts

Positive replies and booked meetings can ping
[Slack](/integrations/slack), [Discord](/integrations/discord),
[Google Chat](/integrations/google-chat) or

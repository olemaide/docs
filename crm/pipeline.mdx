**CRM** has four tabs: **Today**, **Pipeline**, **Contacts** and **Tasks**.

## Contacts are your leads

There is no separate contact list. The **Contacts** tab is the
[leads](/leads/managing-leads) grid, so everything you found, imported or
captured is already there. Companies open in their own panel.

## Views

**Pipeline** shows deals as a **Board**, a **List** or a **Timeline**.
**Reports** has a weighted forecast; admins can tune and save the stage odds.

Save views and filters, turn on **Mine** (in teams), and select deals to move,
assign, archive or delete in bulk, with undo.

## Stages

| Stage | Odds | Stale after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New | 10% | 14 days |
| Qualified | 25% | 21 days |
| In contact | 45% | 21 days |
| Proposal | 70% | 30 days |
| Won | 100% | |
| Lost | 0% | |

Edit them under the gear icon → **Pipelines**: up to 15 stages, each with a
colour, odds and stale days. A pipeline always has exactly one Won, one Lost
and at least one open stage.

Moving a deal to **Lost** asks for a reason: Price, Went with a competitor, No
budget, Bad timing, No response, Not a fit, No decision.

## Where deals come from

- **New deal** on the board
- A reply in the [inbox](/crm/inbox): one click, titled after the thread and
  credited to its campaign
- A contact's panel
- An [AI call](/calls/after-a-call) that ends interested or with a meeting
- The agent and workflows

## Custom fields

Deals and companies take custom fields: Text, Long text, Number, Link, Email,
Phone, Date, Yes/no, Choice, Tags.

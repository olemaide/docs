An **instant quote** is a form that prices the job while people answer it.
They type their address, trace a roof, lawn or fence on a satellite map (or
answer a few questions), and the form ends on a quote with a price range,
line items and a PDF to keep. Every quote is also a response, so the lead
lands in your lists like any other form.

It works wherever forms work: as a link, embedded on any website, and as a
block on ManyPI Sites and landing pages.

## Make one

Open **Forms** and pick **Instant quote: measure on a map** or **Price
calculator**, or start from one of the 24 templates under **Instant quotes**
(roofing, lawn care, fencing, painting, cleaning, solar, moving and more).

Building a site? Ask the Site Agent for it ("add a roof quote to my site"). It picks the
fitting template, puts the quote form on your site and points your "Get a quote"
buttons at it. Set your prices in the form's Pricing tab, then publish it.

An existing form becomes a quote form in its form settings: set the mode to
**Instant quote**. Quote forms get a **Pricing** tab next to Content.

<Warning>
Templates come with example prices. A form with example prices can't be
published: set your own prices, then press **My prices are set** on the
Pricing tab.
</Warning>

## Questions that measure

| Question | What people do |
|---|---|
| Address | Type and pick their address. Optionally limit it to countries or to a service area around you |
| Measure on a map | Trace shapes on satellite imagery of their address |

A **Measure** question has a kind:

| Kind | Traces | For |
|---|---|---|
| Roof | Roof sections, then the pitch | Roofing, solar, gutters |
| Area | Areas | Lawn, turf, driveways, patios, sealing |
| Line | Runs | Fences, gutters, edging, lights |
| Walls | The building outline | Exterior painting, siding |
| Count | Pins | Windows, trees, stumps |
| Volume | Areas, with the depth asked in its own question | Mulch, gravel, concrete |

People can switch between feet and metres on the map. By default the form picks the
units from the property's country, or where the visitor is; set a fixed default in
**Form settings → Language & units**.

**Find my building** draws the building's outline from OpenStreetMap, ready to
adjust. **Let them type instead** lets people who know their numbers skip the
map. Measurements show in feet in the US and in metres elsewhere, or in the
units you set.

### Service areas

Set where you work on the Address question, under **Service areas**. Add as many
areas as you need, each one either:

- **Around a place**: search a town or address (or click the map to drop a pin),
  then set a radius in km or miles
- **Drawn on the map**: click each corner, then the first corner to close it

When someone picks an address outside all of your areas, the form tells them right
away, names the areas you work in ("We work in 30 mi around Kansas City and St.
Louis") and shows them on a small map. Pick an ending under **Further away, go to**
(for example "We don't cover your area yet"), or leave it on **Nowhere** to still let
them send their details.

### Map visits

Satellite imagery is counted in map visits: one visitor opening the map
counts once per 30 minutes.

| | Free | Starter | Pro | Business |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Map visits per month | 25 | 500 | 2,500 | 10,000 |

Past the limit, the map step asks for typed measurements instead, so the form
keeps working.

## Pricing

The **Pricing** tab is where the quote is built. **Try it** on the right shows
the quote for sample answers as you edit.

### Price lines

Each line is **quantity × rate**, and both are formulas. A line can have a
unit (sq ft, m², roofing squares, ft, m, cubic yards, hours, visits, months,
each, flat), its own range, a minimum and a note, and can show only when a
condition matches.

```text
Quantity:  measure.roof.squares * pitch(30)
Rate:      choice.material.rate
```

### Option values

Choices can carry numbers (`rate`, `multiplier`, `hours`, any name you like)
and a picture. Pictures turn the options into cards. Formulas read the picked
option's values as `choice.<question>.<value>`.

### Packages

Up to four packages (Good, Better, Best). People switch between them on the
quote, and formulas read the chosen package's values as `tier.<value>`.

### Quote settings

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| The price reads | A range, exactly, or "from" |
| Show the price | At the end, live while they answer, only after their contact details, or never (you send it) |
| Range below and above | How far the range reaches, in percent |
| Round to | Whole amounts up to the nearest 1,000 |
| Minimum job | The lowest total a quote shows |
| Tax | A rate, a name, added or included |
| Monthly price | APR and months, shown as "from $X/month" |
| Valid for | Days the quote is valid |
| Small print | Shown under the quote and in the PDF |

## Formulas

Formulas use numbers, `+ - * / % ^`, comparisons (`< <= > >= == !=`),
`and`, `or`, `not`, parentheses and these names:

| Name | Is |
|---|---|
| `answer.<ref>` or `<ref>` | A number answer, yes/no as 1 or 0, or how many options were picked |
| `choice.<ref>.<value>` | The picked options' value, added up |
| `picked.<ref>.<option>` | 1 when that option was picked, else 0 |
| `measure.<ref>.<key>` | `area_sqft`, `area_m2`, `squares`, `length_ft`, `length_m`, `perimeter_ft`, `perimeter_m`, `count`, `shapes` |
| `tier.<value>` | The chosen package's value |
| `item.<ref>` | Another price line's amount |
| `var.<key>`, `hidden.<name>` | Logic variables and hidden fields |

| Function | Does |
|---|---|
| `min(a, b, ...)`, `max(a, b, ...)` | Smallest, largest |
| `round(x, step)`, `ceil(x, step)`, `floor(x, step)` | To a step (default 1) |
| `clamp(x, low, high)` | Keeps x between low and high |
| `if(condition, then, otherwise)` | Picks a value |
| `fallback(x, d)` | x, or d when x is 0 |
| `tiers(qty, up_to1, rate1, ..., rate_after)` | A graduated price |
| `band(x, up_to1, value1, ..., value_after)` | The value of the band x falls in |
| `pitch(degrees)`, `slope(rise)` | Roof surface factor for a pitch, or a rise per 12 |
| `abs`, `sqrt`, `pow` | The usual |

Unknown names count as 0 and are flagged before you publish. Dividing by zero
gives 0. Formulas can't run code.

## The quote

The quote ending shows the total, the line items, what they picked, the shapes
they traced and your small print, plus:

- **Download PDF**: a one-page quote in your colours, in Letter or A4
- **Packages** to switch between, when you have them
- Your **booking** and **deposit** sections, when they're on (below)

The server recomputes every quote from the answers when the form is submitted,
so a changed price in the browser is never what you get. The stored quote is
on the response (Results tab and CSV) and in the `form.submitted` event.

**Leads**: create custom lead fields named `quote_low`, `quote_high`,
`quote_total` or `quote_currency` and the quote is saved to them.

**Recall**: use `{{quote.total}}`, `{{quote.low}}`, `{{quote.high}}` and
`{{quote.monthly}}` in endings. Notification emails include the quote.

## Experimental

These three are switched off until ManyPI turns them on for your workspace,
and each is marked **Experimental** in the editor.

### Draft prices with AI

Paste your prices as you have them (a price list, a spreadsheet, an old quote)
and the AI drafts questions and price lines. You see the draft before anything
changes, **Apply** puts it in the editor, and **Undo AI draft** takes it back.

- AI prices are always marked as examples: you confirm them before publishing
- Lines with formulas that don't work are left out and listed, never guessed
- The AI can't touch tax, monthly price, booking or deposits
- Uses AI credits

### Book a visit

People pick a visit time under their quote, from your connected
[Google Calendar](/integrations/google-calendar) or [Cal.com](/integrations/calcom),
and get the invitation.

- Needs **Create leads** on and an email question
- One visit per quote; the time is checked against your calendar again before it's booked
- Set the visit length, how far ahead, the earliest time and the event title
- Fires `form.booking_created`

### Take a deposit

People pay a deposit under their quote, straight into your own Stripe account.

- Connect Stripe under [Integrations](/integrations/overview) with a key that
  can create Checkout Sessions
- Paid plans only
- A percent of the quote (1 to 50%) or a fixed amount, taken on the low end of
  the quote and never more than half of it
- Your deposit terms are shown before people pay, and you confirm you're
  responsible for the deposits
- A deposit is paid only once Stripe says so; fires `form.deposit_paid`

<Note>
ManyPI doesn't receive, hold or split deposits and takes no fee. Refunds,
receipts and taxes are handled in your Stripe account.
</Note>

## Events

| Event | When |
|---|---|
| `form.submitted` | A quote form is completed (with the quote) |
| `form.booking_created` | A visit is booked from a quote (experimental) |
| `form.deposit_paid` | Stripe confirms a deposit (experimental) |

Use them in [webhooks](/integrations/webhooks) and workflow triggers.

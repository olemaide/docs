---
title: "Send quotes to ArcSite"
description: "Turn every instant quote into an ArcSite project, then see approvals and payments on the lead."
---

If you draw and send proposals in [ArcSite](https://www.arcsite.com), ManyPI can
hand each web quote straight over. A homeowner prices their fence or roof on
your website, and a few seconds later the job is waiting in ArcSite with their
details, their address and the quote PDF. When they approve or pay the
proposal, the deal in ManyPI moves to won on its own.

```text
Website quote  →  ManyPI lead + quote  →  ArcSite project  →  proposal approved or paid  →  deal won
```

## What you need

- An [instant quote](/forms/instant-quote) form with your own prices
- An ArcSite plan that includes the Developer API
- The Pro plan or higher in ManyPI (automations on form responses)
- Permission to manage integrations in your ManyPI workspace

## 1. Build the quote form

Open **Forms**, pick **Instant quote: measure on a map** or a template like
**Fence cost calculator**, set your prices on the **Pricing** tab and press
**My prices are set**. Keep the contact question: ArcSite needs at least a
name or an email for the customer.

Turn on **Create leads** on the **Connect** tab, so the quote also becomes a
lead and a deal can follow it.

## 2. Connect ArcSite

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an API token in ArcSite">
    In the ArcSite web app, open **Admin → Developers → API tokens**, create a
    token and copy it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect it in ManyPI">
    Open **Integrations**, then **ArcSite** under CRM. Paste the token and
    enter the **Project owner**: the email of the ArcSite user who should own
    new projects, usually your estimator or yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add tags (optional)">
    Tags such as `Web quote` are added to every new project, next to `ManyPI`
    and the form's name, so you can filter for them in ArcSite.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click **Connect**. ManyPI checks the token before saving it, and stores it
encrypted.

## 3. Add the automation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form's Workflow tab">
    In the form editor, go to **Workflow** and click **+** next to
    **Automations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Send quotes to ArcSite">
    The workflow builder opens with **Someone submits a form** followed by
    **Create an ArcSite project**. The ArcSite step is set to **Automatic**,
    which uses your workspace's ArcSite.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and switch it on">
    From now on, every completed quote is sent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also add **Create an ArcSite project** to any workflow you already
have, after **Someone submits a form**.

## 4. Try it

Fill in your own form from its link, then open ArcSite. You should see a new
project:

| In ArcSite | Comes from |
|---|---|
| Name | The customer's name, their city and a short quote reference |
| Customer | Name, email, phone and company from the answers |
| Job site | The address they picked on the form |
| Tags | `ManyPI`, the form's name, your tags, and `Test` for test responses |
| Drawing | The quote PDF, with the shapes they traced and their measurements |

Open the drawing in ArcSite to refine it, add your products and send the
proposal as usual.

## 5. Get approvals and payments back

So far ManyPI sends to ArcSite. To hear back when a proposal is approved or
paid, add a webhook in ArcSite:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the webhook URL">
    In ManyPI, open **Integrations → ArcSite** and copy the **Webhook URL**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the webhook in ArcSite">
    In ArcSite, open **Admin → Developers → Webhooks**, add a webhook with that
    URL and pick `proposal.status_changed` and `proposal.payment.received`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the secret">
    ArcSite shows a secret for the webhook. Paste it into **Webhook secret** in
    ManyPI and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Now, on the lead in ManyPI:

- Every proposal update shows on the lead's timeline
- An approved or paid proposal marks their open deal **won**
- Payments are recorded on the lead, and paying customers stop getting cold email

Both switches are under **Integrations → ArcSite** if you'd rather not.

## Good to know

- **No doubles.** A quote that is already in ArcSite is never sent twice, even if
  the automation runs again.
- **Quotes without prices** still become a project, just without a drawing.
- **Currency.** ArcSite payments carry no currency, so they are recorded in the
  quote's currency.
- **Other workflows.** The `arcsite.proposal_status_changed` event can start a
  workflow of its own, for example a thank-you email when a proposal is approved.

## If something goes wrong

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| ArcSite refused the token | Create a new token in ArcSite and connect again |
| ArcSite has no user with that email | Set **Project owner** to the email of an ArcSite user |
| The step says ArcSite isn't connected | Connect it under **Integrations**; the next quote goes through |
| Approvals don't show up | Check the webhook URL in ArcSite and that the secret in ManyPI matches |

The workflow's run history shows each attempt and its message. See
[ArcSite](/integrations/arcsite) for every setting.

---
title: "Share as template"
description: "Let anyone view a snapshot of a page, site, form or lead magnet and copy it into their own workspace."
---

A template link (`/remix/<token>`) shows a snapshot of a page, site, form or lead
magnet. Anyone with the link can view it, and anyone with a ManyPI account can
copy it into their own workspace.

## Make a link

| What | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Page | The editor's **Share** tab. |
| Site | **Publish** in the editor, then **Share as template**. |
| Form | The **Share** tab, then **Share as template**. |
| Lead magnet | Its menu in the lead magnets list, then **Share as template**. |

Click **Create link** and copy it. With **Allow copying** off, people only see a
preview. **Expires** sets 7 days, 30 days or never. The panel counts views and
copies.

The link keeps the snapshot from when you made it. Later edits reach it only
when you click **Update snapshot**. Revoking stops the link at once; copies
already made stay.

## What is copied

| Kind | Copied |
| --- | --- |
| Page or site | Its pages, components, look, CMS, translations, and the structure of every form it shows or links to. |
| Form | Its questions, endings, logic and look. |
| Lead magnet | Its name, and its file or the document built in ManyPI. |

Never copied: responses, leads, analytics, domains, integrations, workflows,
webhooks, notification emails, the lists, campaigns and tags new leads go to,
and published state. A form the sharer can't reach is left out, and links to it
are removed.

## Copy a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the link">
    Click **Copy to my workspace**. Signed out, you sign in first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick where it goes">
    Choose the workspace and the brand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the draft">
    The copy opens in its editor as a draft.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A site's own custom code stays off in the copy. The editor shows **Custom code
  from the template is off**: click **Review**, then **Turn on** or **Discard**.
  Custom code on a live site needs a paid plan.
</Note>

## Who can do what

- Making or revoking a link needs **Forms, pages & lead magnets** at **Edit**
  and the **Share templates publicly** action. Changing someone else's link also
  needs **Manage**. See [Roles and permissions](/platform/roles-and-permissions).
- Copying needs **Forms, pages & lead magnets** at **Edit** in the workspace you
  copy into. That workspace's plan limits apply, such as image storage and the
  number of lead magnets.

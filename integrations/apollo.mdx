## What comes over

- Every **saved** contact. Apollo's wider database is not copied, only the
  people you saved.
- Sequences as paused drafts, when the key is a master key
- Contacts who unsubscribed, onto your do-not-contact list

## Get your API key

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings, then Integrations">
    Or go straight to [Apollo's API settings](https://app.apollo.io/#/settings/integrations/api).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open API keys">
    Next to **API**, click **Connect**, then **API keys**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a master key">
    Create a new key, switch on **Set as master key**, and copy it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Without **Set as master key**, Apollo does not let ManyPI read your
  sequences. Contacts still come over.
</Note>

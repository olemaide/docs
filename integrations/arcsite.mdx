ArcSite is drawing and proposal software for contractors. With it connected,
each [instant quote](/forms/instant-quote) becomes an ArcSite project: the
customer and job site come from the answers, and the quote PDF (with the
shapes they traced on the map) is its first drawing. When the proposal is
approved or paid in ArcSite, ManyPI hears about it.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an API token in ArcSite">
    In the ArcSite web app, open **Admin → Developers → API tokens**, create a
    token and copy it. Your ArcSite plan needs the Developer API.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it in ManyPI">
    Open **Integrations**, then **ArcSite** under CRM. Paste the token, enter
    the **Project owner** (the email of the ArcSite user who owns new
    projects) and, if you like, **Tags** for every new project. Click
    **Connect**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

ArcSite connects for the whole workspace, so it needs someone who can manage
integrations. For the whole setup from quote form to won deal, follow
[Send quotes to ArcSite](/forms/quotes-to-arcsite).

## Send quotes to ArcSite

Open the form, go to **Workflow**, add an automation and pick **Send quotes to
ArcSite**. Save it and switch it on. The step is also in any workflow as
**Create an ArcSite project**, after **Someone submits a form**.

Each response becomes one project:

| In ArcSite | From |
|---|---|
| Project name | The customer's name, the city and a short quote reference |
| Customer | Name, email, phone and company from the answers |
| Job site | The address they picked |
| Tags | `ManyPI`, the form's name and your tags (`Test` for test responses) |
| Drawing | The quote PDF |

Running the step again for the same response finds the project it already
made instead of making a second one. A form without prices makes the project
without a drawing.

## Get approvals and payments back

This part is optional.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the webhook URL">
    In ManyPI, open **ArcSite** under Integrations and copy the **Webhook URL**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the webhook in ArcSite">
    In ArcSite, open **Admin → Developers → Webhooks** and add a webhook with
    that URL for `proposal.status_changed` and `proposal.payment.received`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the secret in ManyPI">
    Copy the webhook's secret from ArcSite, paste it into **Webhook secret**
    in ManyPI and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Then:

- A proposal update adds a line to the lead's timeline
- An approved or paid proposal marks their open deal won
- A payment is recorded on the lead, like payments from Stripe
- `arcsite.proposal_status_changed` is available to [webhooks](/integrations/webhooks) and workflow triggers

Deliveries without a valid signature, or older than five minutes, are refused.

## Settings

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Stop cold email to paying customers | On: their campaigns end and they go on do-not-contact |
| Mark their open deal won | On: when a proposal is approved or paid |
| Project owner and tags | Under **More settings**, changeable without reconnecting |

ArcSite payments don't carry a currency, so they are recorded in the quote's
currency (or USD without a quote).

## Troubleshooting

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| ArcSite refused the token | Create a new token in ArcSite and connect again |
| ArcSite has no user with that email | Set **Project owner** to the email of an ArcSite user |
| Nothing arrives from ArcSite | Check the webhook URL and that the secret in ManyPI is the one ArcSite shows |

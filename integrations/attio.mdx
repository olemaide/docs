## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an access token">
    In Attio, open **Workspace settings → Developers** (or go straight to
    [developer settings](https://app.attio.com/_/settings/developers)) and
    click **New access token**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it access">
    Grant read-write access to **records** and **notes**, and read access to
    **object configuration**. Copy the token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it in ManyPI">
    Open **Integrations** in the sidebar, click **Attio**, paste the token, and
    click **Connect**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<CrmActions name="Attio" />

Leads go to Attio as people.

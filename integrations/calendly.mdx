## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a personal access token">
    In Calendly, open **Integrations & apps → API and webhooks** (or go
    straight to [API and webhooks](https://calendly.com/integrations/api_webhooks)).
    Under **Personal access tokens**, click **Generate new token** and copy
    it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it in ManyPI">
    Open **Integrations** in the sidebar, click **Calendly**, paste the token,
    and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On Calendly's Standard plan and above, ManyPI registers a webhook and bookings
arrive straight away. Calendly's free plan does not offer webhooks, so ManyPI
checks for new bookings every few minutes instead.

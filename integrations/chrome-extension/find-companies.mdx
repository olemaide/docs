**Find companies** turns a description into a list of company websites and
scans the ones you pick.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search">
    Type who you are looking for, for example *marketing agencies in Austin*.
    Pick a country with the globe button if you want to narrow it down.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the sites">
    ManyPI returns company websites, with directories, review sites,
    marketplaces and LinkedIn left out. Untick any you do not want. **More
    results** loads the next page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan">
    **Scan sites** reads each ticked site, two at a time. It keeps going with
    the panel closed; the toolbar icon shows progress and Chrome tells you
    when it is done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cost

Each results page costs **1 crawl credit**, whether or not it held new
companies. Scanning the sites uses no credits.

## Where the search runs

The search runs on ManyPI's servers, never in your browser, so you are not
asked to solve CAPTCHAs. The extension only opens the company sites you
ticked.

For automation, the same search is available as
[Find company websites](/api-reference/leads/find-company-sites) in the API.

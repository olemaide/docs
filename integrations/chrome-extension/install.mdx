<Note>
  ManyPI Email Finder is coming soon to the Chrome Web Store.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add it to Chrome">
    Find **ManyPI: Email Finder & Lead Extractor** in the Chrome Web Store and click **Add to Chrome**. Pin
    it from the puzzle icon in the toolbar so it is one click away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the side panel">
    Click the ManyPI icon. The panel opens next to the page you are on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with ManyPI">
    Click **Sign in with ManyPI** and approve the connection. Leads you send
    go to the workspace and brand you work in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use an API key instead

In the extension's settings, open **Advanced** and paste a ManyPI API key with
the **Write** permission. Create one under **API Access** in your profile
menu.

## Site access

The first time you scan a site, Chrome asks to let ManyPI read it. You can
allow one site at a time; **Find companies** asks once for all sites, because
it scans sites you pick from a list.

## Sign out

**Settings → Sign out** forgets the sign-in on this browser. Your scanned
contacts stay in the browser until you clear them. To revoke the connection
itself, disconnect it in **Apps & integrations** in ManyPI.

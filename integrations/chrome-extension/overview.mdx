**ManyPI for Chrome** reads a company's website in your browser and pulls out
the people behind it: names, titles, email addresses and phone numbers. Send
them to ManyPI as leads in one click, or export a CSV.

## What it costs

Scanning pages and websites uses **no AI credits**: the extension reads the
pages in your browser. **Find companies** uses ManyPI's search and costs
**1 crawl credit per results page**.

## Requirements

- Chrome 116 or newer, or another Chromium browser (Edge, Brave, Arc).
- A ManyPI account. Every scan needs you to be signed in.

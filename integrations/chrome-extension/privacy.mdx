| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Reads** | Only pages you ask it to scan, and only while you are signed in. |
| **Never reads** | Your browsing history, other tabs, LinkedIn or Meta pages. |
| **Stores** | Your sign-in and scanned contacts, in this browser only. |
| **Sends** | Contacts you choose to send, to your ManyPI workspace. Searches from Find companies go to ManyPI. Nothing goes anywhere else. |
| **Runs** | No AI on your pages, no analytics, no code loaded from the internet. |

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Side panel | The extension's interface. |
| Active tab, scripting | Read the page you ask it to scan. |
| Site access | Read a whole site when you scan it; asked per site, or once for Find companies. |
| Storage | Keep your sign-in and contacts in this browser. |
| Identity | Sign in with ManyPI. |
| Offscreen, alarms | Keep a scan running when the panel is closed. |
| Notifications | Tell you when a long scan finishes. |

## Remove your data

**⋯ → Clear** deletes the contacts stored in the extension. **Settings →
Sign out** forgets the sign-in. Uninstalling removes everything the extension
stored.

Open a company's website, open the ManyPI panel, and pick one:

- **Scan website** reads the site: up to **30 pages**, or **100** on Deep
  (Settings → Scanning). Contact, team, Impressum, about and leadership pages
  come first, in English and German. It stops early once those are read and
  new pages stop turning up new people.
- **This page only** reads the page you are on, including pages built with
  JavaScript.

Contacts appear while it reads. If pages are left when it stops, **Scan more
pages** carries on where it ended.

## What you get

Each contact shows a name, title, email and phone number where the site
published them, grouped by company.

| Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Personal** | A person's own address, found on the site. |
| **Inbox** | A shared address such as info@ or hello@. |
| **Guess** | Worked out from the company's naming pattern (for example `first.last@`). Not confirmed: verify it before you send. |

Titles like *Dr.*, *Mag.* or *MBA* are taken out of names and kept with the
contact, so guesses use the person's real name.

## What it reads, and what it skips

- It respects each site's `robots.txt`.
- It skips pages behind a login and cookie walls, and says how many.
- It never opens LinkedIn or Meta pages.
- An agency's address in a site's footer is filed under that agency, not
  under the company you are scanning.

<Tip>
  Guessed addresses are a starting point. Run them through
  [email verification](/leads/email-validation) before a campaign.
</Tip>

Click **Send to ManyPI** to save every contact not sent yet, or use a
company's **Send** button for just that company. Saved contacts get a check
mark.

- Leads land in the workspace and brand you work in, shown at the top of the
  panel.
- Someone already in your leads is merged, not duplicated.
- Each send appears in **Leads** as a batch called **Chrome extension**.

By default, guessed addresses are sent as name and company only. Turn on
**Send guessed emails too** in the extension's settings to include them.

## Export instead

**⋯ → Export CSV** downloads the contacts, or one company's from its own menu.
**Open table** shows every column on a full page, with sorting and search.

## If a send fails

The extension keeps unsent contacts and tries again. Offline sends wait for
your connection. If your plan's lead limit is reached, the panel says so and
nothing is lost.

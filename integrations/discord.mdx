## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the server's integrations">
    In Discord, open **Server Settings → Integrations**. You need the Manage
    Webhooks permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a webhook">
    Click **Webhooks**, then **New Webhook**, and pick the channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy its URL">
    Click **Copy Webhook URL**. It starts with
    `https://discord.com/api/webhooks/`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it in ManyPI">
    Open **Integrations** in the sidebar, click **Discord**, paste the URL, and
    click **Connect**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---
title: "Excel"
description: "Import an Excel workbook as leads, with columns matched for you."
icon: "/images/logos/excel.svg"
---

There is nothing to connect. Drop an `.xlsx` file anywhere on **Leads**, or
open **Leads → Import** and pick it.

- The first sheet with rows in it is imported.
- Columns are matched the way a CSV's are: "Company Name", "companyName" and
  "company" all land in Company.
- Columns ManyPI does not recognize are kept as custom variables, so you can
  use them in emails.
- A lead with the same email as one you already have is merged, not
  duplicated.

The same importer takes CSV, TSV and JSON files, and rows pasted straight out
of Excel. See [Managing leads](/leads/managing-leads).

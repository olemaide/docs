## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your API key">
    In Freshdesk, click your profile picture, then **Profile settings**. Under
    **Your API Key** on the right, click **View API Key** and copy it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the connection">
    Open **Integrations** in ManyPI, click **Freshdesk**, and enter:

    - **Helpdesk address**: `acme` if your help desk is `acme.freshdesk.com`
    - **API key**: the key you copied
  </Step>
</Steps>

Webhooks in Google Chat are available on Google Workspace accounts.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the space">
    In Google Chat, open the space and click its name at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a webhook">
    Choose **Apps & integrations**, then **Add webhooks**. Give it a name (for
    example, ManyPI) and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the URL">
    Copy the webhook URL. It starts with
    `https://chat.googleapis.com/v1/spaces/`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it in ManyPI">
    Open **Integrations** in the sidebar, click **Google Chat**, paste the URL,
    and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

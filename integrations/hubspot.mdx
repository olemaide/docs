## Connect

Open **Integrations** in the sidebar, click **HubSpot**, then **Connect**, and
approve ManyPI in HubSpot.

If ManyPI asks for a token instead, connect with a private app:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Private Apps">
    In HubSpot, open **Settings → Integrations → Private Apps** (or go
    straight to [private apps](https://app.hubspot.com/l/private-apps)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a private app">
    Under **Scopes**, tick `crm.objects.contacts.read` and
    `crm.objects.contacts.write`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the token">
    Create the app, copy its access token (it starts with `pat-`), and paste
    it in ManyPI.
  </Step>
</Steps>

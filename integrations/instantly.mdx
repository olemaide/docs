## What comes over

- Every lead with its custom variables
- Campaigns as paused drafts, steps and A/B variants included
- Your block list, bounces and unsubscribes, onto your do-not-contact list
- Leads not contacted yet, queued in the new campaign

## Get your API key

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings in Instantly">
    Or go straight to [Integrations settings](https://app.instantly.ai/app/settings/integrations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Integrations, then API Keys" />
  <Step title="Create a key">
    Click **Create API Key**, give it the scope `all:read` (or `all`), and copy
    it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Use an **API V2** key. Keys made for Instantly's old V1 API do not work.
</Warning>

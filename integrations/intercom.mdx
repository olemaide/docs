## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Developer Hub">
    In Intercom, open the [Developer Hub](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/_/developer-hub)
    and create an app, or open one you already have.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the access token">
    Go to **Configure → Authentication** and copy the access token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it in ManyPI">
    Open **Integrations** in the sidebar, click **Intercom**, paste the token,
    and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

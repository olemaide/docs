## What comes over

- Every lead with its custom variables, icebreakers included
- The email steps of your campaigns, as paused drafts
- Unsubscribed people and domains, onto your do-not-contact list

LinkedIn and call steps are listed in the import summary so you know they
existed, but only email steps are copied.

## Get your API key

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings in lemlist">
    Click your profile picture in [lemlist](https://app.lemlist.com/), then
    **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Integrations tab" />
  <Step title="Generate a key">
    Click **Generate a new API key** and copy it. lemlist shows it once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

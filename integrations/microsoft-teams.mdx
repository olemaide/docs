Teams receives messages through a **Workflows** webhook. ManyPI sends them as
Adaptive Cards, so they show a title, the details and an **Open in ManyPI**
button.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Workflows on the channel">
    In Teams, open the channel's **...** menu and choose **Workflows**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the webhook template">
    Choose **Post to a channel when a webhook request is received**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the URL">
    Finish the steps and copy the URL Teams gives you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it in ManyPI">
    Open **Integrations** in the sidebar, click **Microsoft Teams**, paste the
    URL, and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

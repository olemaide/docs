## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your API token">
    In Pipedrive, click your profile, then **Personal preferences**, and open
    the **API** tab (or go straight to [API settings](https://app.pipedrive.com/settings/api)).
    Copy your personal API token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it in ManyPI">
    Open **Integrations** in the sidebar, click **Pipedrive**, paste the token,
    and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<CrmActions name="Pipedrive" />

Leads go to Pipedrive as people.

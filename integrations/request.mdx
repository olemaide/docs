If a tool you use is not on the [Integrations](/integrations/overview) list,
email us. We read every request, and the tools people ask for most are the
ones we build next.

## What helps us most

- **The tool**, with a link if it is not well known.
- **What you want to happen.** For example: "when a lead replies positively,
  create a deal in our CRM", or "import my contacts from it".
- **How you work around it today**, if you do.

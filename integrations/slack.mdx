ManyPI posts to Slack through an incoming webhook, which belongs to one
channel.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Slack app">
    Go to [api.slack.com/apps](https://api.slack.com/apps), click **Create New
    App**, then **From scratch**, and pick your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on incoming webhooks">
    Open **Incoming Webhooks** and switch it on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a webhook">
    Click **Add New Webhook to Workspace**, pick the channel, and copy the URL.
    It starts with `https://hooks.slack.com/services/`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it in ManyPI">
    Open **Integrations** in the sidebar, click **Slack**, paste the URL, and
    click **Connect**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What comes over

- Every lead in every campaign, with its custom fields
- Campaigns as paused drafts, variants included
- Bounced, unsubscribed and do-not-contact leads, onto your do-not-contact list

## Get your API key

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings in Smartlead">
    Or go straight to [your profile settings](https://app.smartlead.ai/app/settings/profile).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the API Key section">
    Click **Activate API** if you have not used the API before.
  </Step>

</Steps>

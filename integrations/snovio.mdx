## What comes over

- Every prospect in your lists
- The email steps of your campaigns, as paused drafts
- Your do-not-email lists, onto your do-not-contact list

## Get your API credentials

Snov.io uses two values, a **User ID** and a **Secret**. Paste both.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account settings in Snov.io">
    Or go straight to [the API tab](https://app.snov.io/account/api).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the API tab" />
  <Step title="Copy both values">
    Copy your **User ID** and **API Secret**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A campaign holds its own emails under **Outreach → Campaigns → Editor**.
Besides emails, a sequence can wait, branch and act on the lead.

## Step types

| Step | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Email | A message, as a new thread or a reply under the first email |
| Reply bait | A short question. A reply to it does not stop the sequence. |
| Wait | Pause before the next step |
| If/else | Branch on replied, reply sentiment, or opened |
| Only continue if | Stop the lead here unless a condition holds |
| Update the lead | Set a field or status |
| Move to another campaign | Hand the lead to another sequence |
| Webhook | Send the lead to your URL |

## Variables

`{{first_name}}`, `{{last_name}}`, `{{full_name}}`, `{{company}}`,
`{{title}}`, `{{email}}`, `{{phone}}`, `{{domain}}`, `{{location}}`, plus
`{{unsubscribe_url}}`, `{{signature}}` and `{{booking_link}}`.

Custom columns: `{{custom.industry}}`. Fallbacks after a bar:
`{{first_name|there}}`. Spellings from other tools (`FirstName`) are
recognised.

## AI personalization

| | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Drafting an email or a sequence with AI | Free |
| A personal line written for each lead as it sends | AI credits, by tokens used |

If credits run out, the campaign keeps sending the email as written.

## Sending window

Off by default. Switched on, it starts at Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00, in your
time zone or **each lead's**. Each campaign also has a daily limit and a
priority.

## Tracking

| | Default | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opens | On | A hidden image; slightly lowers inbox placement |
| Clicks | Off | Switch on per campaign |
| UTM tags | Off | Added to your links |

## Replies

Choose **Stop when they reply**, or **Let AI read the reply first** so an
out-of-office keeps the sequence going and an unsubscribe stops and
suppresses it. See [Replies](/outreach/replies).

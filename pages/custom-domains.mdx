---
title: "Custom domains"
description: "Serve your landing pages, sites and forms from your own domain."
---

One domain serves both pages and forms. Open **Forms & Pages → Domains** (or
**Share → Manage domains** on a form) and click **Add domain**.

## DNS records

| Type | Name | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CNAME` | `go.yourcompany.com` | `domains.usemanypi.eu` |
| `TXT` | `_manypi.go.yourcompany.com` | `manypi-verify=…` (shown in the app) |

For a root domain (`yourcompany.com`), use an `ALIAS` record (also called
`ANAME` or a flattened `CNAME`) to `domains.usemanypi.eu` instead of the
`CNAME`. Your DNS provider may be asked for an extra `TXT` record to validate
the certificate; the app shows it if so.

The certificate is issued automatically.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Waiting for DNS | The records aren't visible yet |
| Verifying | Checking the `TXT` record |
| Issuing certificate | Almost there |
| Live | Serving your pages and forms |
| Failed | Check the records and click **Check now** |
| Paused on your plan | Over the plan's domain limit |

Pending domains are checked on every run, live ones daily. **Check now**
checks straight away.

## Limits

| | Starter | Pro | Business |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Domains | 1 | 3 | 10 |

[Websites](/pages/website-plans) get 2 domains each on top of these.

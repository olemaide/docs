**Forms & Pages → Landing pages** lists your pages with their status:
**Live**, **Live, changes not published**, or **Draft**.

## Create a page

Click **New page**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    Write a sentence or two under **Describe your page**, or start from an idea
    like **Product waitlist** or **Book a call**. Optionally pick your type of
    business, and a **Form** to build the page around.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it write">
    The page opens in the editor straight away, and the AI writes it section by
    section.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Or start from **Templates** (one per type of business: food and drink, shops,
services and more) or a **Blank page**. From a form, **Share → Landing page**
builds a page around it.

## Edit

The toolbar has **Add section**, **Design**, a **Desktop / Phone** switch,
**Preview**, **Page settings** and undo/redo.

| Section | Layouts |
| --- | --- |
| Navigation | Always at the top |
| Hero | Text, Image, Form, Cover |
| Features, Text and image, Gallery, Testimonials, Menu and prices, Form, FAQ, Call to action, Visit us | |
| Footer | Always at the bottom |

Hover a section to move it, duplicate it, remove it, or give it a **Plain**,
**Soft** or **Accent** background. The **Ask AI** bar on the canvas changes
the page in plain words:

```text
Make the hero shorter and add an FAQ about pricing and delivery times.
```

**Design** has nine themes (Snow, Paper, Espresso, Sky, Sage, Terracotta,
Butter, Blush, Ink) and sets the accent colour, type (Clean, Classic, Bold,
Mono), buttons (Square, Rounded, Pill) and heading alignment.

## Page settings

| Setting | |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Tab icon on paid plans |
| Address | The page's path |
| Search and sharing | Title (60 characters), description (160), picture for shared links |
| Take offline | **Unpublish** |

A page on your [own domain](/pages/custom-domains) can be indexed by search
engines. The shared `usemanypi.eu` address never is.

## Publish

Click **Publish**. Later edits stay in the draft until **Publish changes**.
Publishing is blocked while:

- a bracketed placeholder is still on the page,
- a form section has no form,
- a form on the page isn't published, or belongs to another brand.

Form responses on the page become leads in the form's lists, like any other
[form](/forms/overview).

| Address | |
| --- | --- |
| Default | `https://<workspace host>.usemanypi.eu/p/<page id>` |
| Your domain | `https://go.yourcompany.com/<slug>` |

The **Share** tab has the link, a QR code, a [preview link](/agencies/sharing)
and your domain.

A site can also go live on a Free workspace with its own website plan.

## Rules

- **Extra languages**: $9 a month each, up to 12.
- **Busy months**: a site keeps showing up to 2× its visits, then pauses.
- **Domains**: 2 per site (root and `www`), not counted against the
  workspace's domains.
- **Ending coverage**: 14 days of grace before an uncovered site goes offline.
- Only the workspace **owner** can buy or change website plans.

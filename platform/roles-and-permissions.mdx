Every member of a workspace has a **role**. A role sets a level for each area of
the product, and switches on the actions that spend credits or reach people.

## Roles

Thirteen roles, grouped by job. To see exactly what one allows, open the profile
menu, click your workspace, go to **Roles** and click the role.

| Role | For | Seat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including the plan and deleting the workspace. | Paid |
| Admin | Everything except the plan and deleting the workspace. | Paid |
| Manager | Runs the work and the team, without workspace settings. | Paid |
| Member | Works in every area and spends credits. No team or billing. | Paid |
| Sales | Leads, campaigns, inbox, CRM and calls. | Paid |
| Marketing | Forms, pages, campaigns, workflows and social. | Paid |
| Research | Finds and qualifies leads. Never sends. | Paid |
| Developer | API, MCP, webhooks, scrapers and integrations. | Paid |
| Finance | Plan, invoices, usage and credit packs. No work areas. | Paid |
| Viewer | Sees everything in their brands, changes nothing. | Free |
| Client | Your client: sees results in their brand, changes nothing. | Free |
| Client editor | Your client, who also answers replies and works deals. | Paid |
| Freelancer | Does the work in their brands, without team or settings. | Paid |

Client, Client editor and Freelancer always work in
[chosen brands](#brands-and-clients).

## Levels and actions

Each area, such as Leads, Campaigns, Inbox, CRM or Billing, gets one level:

| Level | Allows |
| --- | --- |
| No access | Nothing. The area is hidden from the sidebar. |
| View | Seeing it. |
| Edit | Creating and changing things. |
| Manage | Also the area's settings and other people's work. |

Actions are switched on one by one, whatever the levels say:

| Group | Actions |
| --- | --- |
| Spending credits | Use AI, search the database and map, run scrapers, verify email addresses |
| Reaching people | Launch campaigns and send, reply from the inbox, place AI calls |
| Going public | Publish forms and pages, share templates publicly |
| Data | Import, export and download, delete in bulk |
| Automation & usage | Run and switch on workflows, allocate usage |

## Rules

- Nobody can give a role with more rights than their own, or remove someone who
  has more. Roles and levels above yours are locked.
- The owner can't be changed or removed by anyone else.
- Inviting and removing people needs **Team** at **Edit**. Changing roles needs
  **Team** at **Manage** (Owner, Admin, or a custom role with it).
- Areas a role can't open are left out of the sidebar. A link into one shows a
  page saying it isn't part of the role.

## Guest seats

A role that only views, and can't spend credits, send, call or publish, takes no
paid seat. Viewer and Client are guest roles, and so is any custom role that
follows the same rule. Guest seats are free on every paid plan, up to 100 per
workspace.

## Only their own records

By default everyone sees every record in the brands they reach. On Pro and
Business, a member can be limited to their own: open the menu next to them under
**Members** and tick **Only their own records**. On Business, a custom role can
make this its default. A member's own setting wins over the role's.

| Record | Theirs when |
| --- | --- |
| Lead | They own it, or it has no owner and they added it. |
| Deal | They own it or created it. |
| Task | It is assigned to them or they created it. |
| Conversation | It is assigned to them, or it is with one of their leads. |
| Campaign, chat | They created it. |

Owners and admins always see every record.

## API keys, MCP, the agent and workflows

Keys, OAuth tokens and the agent act as the person behind them, and are held to
that person's role on every call.

- A [key](/platform/api-keys) never does more than its owner's role allows,
  whatever permissions it carries. Narrow the role and the key narrows with it.
- An [MCP connection](/mcp/authentication) gets the role of the person who
  signed in.
- The agent only gets the tools the role allows. A Viewer's agent can't send
  email.
- A [workflow](/agent/workflows) runs as its owner. Its **Find leads**, **Check
  their website**, **Ask AI**, **Run an agent** and **Have AI call them** steps
  check the owner's role every time they run, and fail with the reason in the
  run log if it no longer allows them.

A call the role doesn't allow returns `403`:

```json
{
  "error": "Your role (Sales) can't export and download. Ask an admin.",
  "code": "permission_denied",
  "area": "leads",
  "action": "export"
}
```

`action` is there when the role has the area but not the action.

## Brands and clients

On Pro and Business, people can be limited to some brands. They then see only
those brands, everywhere in the app. Owners and admins always reach every brand.

- **When inviting**: pick the brands next to the role.
- **Later**: open the menu next to the member under **Members**, then
  **Brand access**.

Both show once the workspace has more than one brand. An invite for Client,
Client editor or Freelancer needs at least one brand.

### A different role per brand

On Pro and Business, a member can have another role in a specific brand, for
example Manager in "Client A" and Viewer everywhere else. It applies while they
work in that brand. Set it from the menu next to the member under **Members**,
then **Brand roles**. **Workspace role** clears it.

A brand role decides the work in that brand. Team, billing, workspace settings,
developer access and **Allocate usage** always follow the workspace role.

## Who can use a connection

When you connect an integration, pick under **Who can use it**. Change it later
in the connection's settings. The choice shows when your role allows more than
one.

| Setting | Used by | Who can pick it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Everyone | Everyone in the workspace. | **Integrations** at **Manage**, without a brand limit. |
| This brand | People who work in the brand. | **Integrations** at **Manage** in that brand. A brand role counts. |
| Only me | Only the person who connected it. | **Integrations** at **Edit**. |

Admins see a teammate's **Only me** connection so they can disconnect it, but
can't use it. Automations, such as adding positive replies to your CRM, never
use an **Only me** connection. Payment providers and the lead intake URL always
serve the whole workspace.

[Inboxes](/outreach/inboxes) belong to a brand. The person who connected one can
open it under **Email accounts** and turn on **Only me** under **Sending**. Then
only they see it, pick it for campaigns and read its replies.

## Usage limits and budgets

Both cap AI credits, crawls and email validations per day, week or month, on top
of the workspace's own credits.

### Per member

On Pro and Business, cap what one person starts themselves. Open the menu next
to the member under **Members**, then **Usage limit**.

- Work they start past a limit gets `429` with `code: "member_limit"` and
  `resets_at`. An agent run already going stops.
- Automations running in the background, such as personalization, reply
  analysis and AI steps in workflows, count against the workspace, never against
  one person's limit.
- Owners are never limited.
- Only roles with **Allocate usage** can set limits, and never above their own.

### Per brand

On Business, give each brand its own budget. Open the brand (in the Workspaces
dialog, **Brands**, then the brand), then **Settings**, then **Budget and
usage**.

- A brand's budget counts everything done in it, automations included. Past it,
  work in the brand gets `429` with `code: "brand_limit"`.
- Below the budget is the brand's usage per month: AI credits, crawls,
  validations, emails sent and call minutes. **Download CSV** exports it, to
  bill the client.

| | Who |
| --- | --- |
| See it | The owner, and roles with **Allocate usage** or **Billing** at **View** |
| Download it | The same, with **Export and download** |
| Set the budget | The owner, and roles with **Allocate usage** and **Brands** at **Manage** |

Client, Client editor and Freelancer never see a brand's budget.

## Removing a member

Open the menu next to them under **Members** and choose **Remove**. If they own
work, the dialog lists it and asks what happens to it:

| Option | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Hand over to | A teammate takes over their campaigns, workflows, agents, brand inboxes, shared app connections, MCP connectors, deals, tasks and leads. |
| Pause what runs in their name | With nobody to hand it to: their sending campaigns pause, active workflows switch off, running agents pause and personal inboxes stop sending. |
| Disconnect their personal apps | Their **Only me** connections are disconnected. |

Personal inboxes and connections never move to someone else. Their API keys
stay theirs, but no longer reach this workspace.

## Activity log

Open the profile menu, click your workspace, then **Activity**. It lists, newest
first:

- role, brand access, brand role, usage limit and own-records changes,
- invites sent and withdrawn, and people who joined, left, were removed or
  handed over,
- custom roles created, changed or deleted,
- ownership offered, accepted or withdrawn,
- apps connected, disconnected, or opened to more or fewer people.

Pro shows the last 30 days, Business the last year. It is recorded on every
plan. People with **Team** at **Edit** or more can see it.

## Custom roles

On Business, copy a role under **Roles**, or click **New role**, and set each
area and action yourself. A custom role can also be limited to chosen brands,
and can make **Only their own records** its default.

Built-in roles can't be edited. A workspace can have up to 50 custom roles, and
one can't be deleted while members still have it.

## Handing over the workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Offer it">
    As the owner, open the menu next to a member under **Members** and choose
    **Make owner**. Guests can't be offered the workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They accept">
    They open the workspace from their profile menu and accept or decline. The
    offer lasts 7 days, and you can withdraw it until then.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    They own the workspace, its plan and its billing. You stay on as an admin.
  </Step>
</Steps>

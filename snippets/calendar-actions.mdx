## What happens on a booking

- The meeting shows up on the lead's timeline.
- **Pause their campaigns when someone books** (on by default) stops the
  sequence, so nobody gets a follow-up after saying yes.
- **Add new bookers as leads** (on by default) creates a lead for someone who
  books without being one yet.
- Workflows and [webhooks](/integrations/webhooks) can react to it through
  the `meeting.booked` event.

## Your booking link

Under **More settings**, pick the booking page {name} should use. Then write
`{{booking_link}}` in any email, or insert it from **Sign off** in the email
editor. Each send fills in that page's address.

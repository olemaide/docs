## What you can do

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Import contacts" icon="file-import">
    Open the {name} connection under **Integrations** and click **Import {name}
    contacts**. Choose what they are:

    - **Prospects to reach out to** become leads you can add to campaigns.
    - **Existing customers** go on your do-not-contact list, so nobody you
      already work with gets cold email.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Send leads" icon="paper-plane">
    In **Leads**, tick the leads you want (or filter the list), open
    **Export**, and click **Send to {name}**. Leads already in {name} with the
    same email are updated, not duplicated.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Add leads automatically" icon="bolt">
    Two switches on the connection, both on by default:

    - **Add leads who reply positively**, with their reply as a note.
    - **Add leads who book a meeting**, with the meeting as a note.

    These run on paid plans.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What gets posted

Two switches on the connection, both on by default:

- **Post when someone replies positively**: who replied, a one-line summary
  of their reply, and the campaign.
- **Post when someone books a meeting**: who booked, the meeting and its time,
  and the calendar it came from.

Each message has an **Open in ManyPI** link to the conversation.
Notifications run on paid plans.

Click **Test** on the connection to send a sample message to {where}.

<Tip>
  Need every reply, bounce or deal change, or a format of your own? Use a
  [webhook](/integrations/webhooks) instead and shape the message yourself.
</Tip>

## Import contacts

Open the {name} connection under **Integrations** and click **Import
contacts**. Choose what they are:

- **Existing customers** go on your do-not-contact list. This is the usual
  choice for a support tool: the people in it already pay you, and cold email
  is the fastest way to annoy them.
- **Prospects to reach out to** become leads you can add to campaigns.

Run it again whenever you want to pick up new contacts. Addresses already on
the list are skipped.

## Run the import

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the wizard">
    In ManyPI, open **Integrations** in the sidebar, click **Switch from
    another tool**, and pick {tool}.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste your key">
    ManyPI checks it and scans the account. Nothing is imported yet: you see
    how many leads, campaigns and do-not-contact entries it found.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to bring">
    Everything starts ticked. Untick **Recreate the campaigns**, **Continue
    where they left off** or **Bring the do-not-contact list** to leave any of
    them behind.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    The import runs on ManyPI's servers. Close the window whenever you like;
    **Integrations** shows its progress until it is done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After the import

- Campaigns arrive as **drafts**. Nothing sends until you connect an inbox
  and launch. See [Inboxes and warmup](/outreach/inboxes).
- With **Continue where they left off**, leads {tool} had not emailed yet are
  queued in their new campaign, so the sequence picks up when you launch.
- A lead with the same email as one you already have is merged, not
  duplicated. Imported leads count toward your plan's lead limit.
- Custom variables become lead fields with the same names, so the copied
  emails keep their personalization.
- Your key is stored encrypted while the import runs and erased when it ends.
